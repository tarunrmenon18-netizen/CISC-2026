01 / Call for Papers

Submit original research on open-source circuits

We invite researchers, academicians and engineers to submit original technical papers on open-source electronic circuits, FPGA platforms and embedded computing. Accepted papers are published in the conference proceedings with ISBN.

IEEE paper templates (opens in a new tab)

03 / About the conference

Open hardware and silicon ecosystems

Organised by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at the Federal Institute of Science And Technology (FISAT) in association with e-Yantra, CISC 2026 brings together domain experts, scholars and VLSI engineers to advance open-source hardware innovation.

Academic publication

Peer-reviewed, accepted and presented papers are published in the official proceedings with ISBN.

Hands-on workshops

Intensive sessions on AMD Vivado FPGA synthesis, silicon reverse engineering and drone hardware development.

Previous edition

CISC 2025 publication

Selected papers were published in Peer-reviewed & Refereed journal “International Journal of Innovative Research in Electrical, Electronics, Instrumentation and Control Engineering” as Vol. 13, Special Issue 1, September 2025, with ISSN Online 2321-2004 and ISSN Print 2321-5526.

04 / Technical focus areas

Conference Tracks

6 primary research domains

TRACK 01 VLSI / RTL

Digital Systems & FPGA

Verilog/VHDL, open-source synthesis, RISC-V cores, AMD Vivado design suites and custom ASIC flows.

TRACK 02 RTOS / EDGE

IoT & Embedded Systems

Microcontroller architectures, FreeRTOS, low-power hardware nodes, edge sensor networks and firmware security.

TRACK 03 ROS 2 / UAV

Robotics & Autonomous Control

ROS 2, e-Yantra embedded hardware platforms, drone development and automated motor controllers.

TRACK 04 SDR / RF

Communication Systems

Software defined radio, open-source RF tools, wireless sensor stacks and 5G/6G physical layer design.

TRACK 05 TINYML / NPU

AI / Machine Learning Hardware

Neuromorphic computing, TinyML, hardware accelerators for deep learning and vector processor arrays.

TRACK 06 EDA / DSP

Signal Processing & EDA

KiCad, Ngspice, OpenLANE, Yosys, digital signal filtering and hardware reverse engineering.

08 / Common queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Can delegates attend online in hybrid mode? +

Yes. CISC 2026 runs in hybrid mode. Outstation participants whose papers are accepted can present virtually via Google Meet.

Which format should my paper follow? +

Use the IEEE two-column conference format, 6 to 12 pages, in English. Word and LaTeX templates are linked in the Call for Papers section.

When will the presentation schedule be sent? +

Individual session slots and virtual track links will be e-mailed to registered authors by September 05, 2026.

How do I pay the registration fee? +

Open the registration form, choose your category and pay by UPI. Then enter the transaction ID in the form so the organising team can verify your payment.

Will accommodation be provided at FISAT? +

Hostel accommodation can be arranged for participants on prior request, for a nominal charge. Please contact the organising team early.
